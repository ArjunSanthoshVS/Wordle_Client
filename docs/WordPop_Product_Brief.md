## WordPop – Feature Plan (User-Friendly Brief)

### What is coming to WordPop
We’re leveling up WordPop with fun ways to express yourself, track progress, compete with friends, and earn playful rewards — all while keeping the quick, satisfying daily puzzle you love.

### Your Profile
- Create a username and pick a cute avatar (emoji or icon)
- Show off your favorite badges on your profile
- Optional short bio (keep it fun!)

### Stats & Progress
- See your daily streak and best streak ever
- Win rate and average number of guesses
- “Hardest word you solved” highlight
- Guess distribution chart (how often you win in 2, 3, 4… tries)

### Achievements & Badges
- 7‑Day Streak 🏅
- Solved in 1 Try 🔥
- Hard Mode Master 🧠
- 100 Words Cracked 🎉
- More seasonal/event badges later (limited-time collectibles!)

### Daily Streak Rewards
- Come back every day to keep your streak alive
- Earn small daily rewards (coins/points)
- Optional “streak saver” in special cases so you don’t lose a long streak

### Compete & Play With Others
- Leaderboards: Global and Friends-only, plus Weekly/Monthly resets
- Challenges: Send a word challenge link to your friends
- Multiplayer:
  - Head‑to‑head: same word, fastest to solve wins
  - Team mode: collaborate to solve within a time limit

### Share Your Wins
- Share your result as a satisfying colored grid (like classic Wordle)
- Include your guesses, streak, and a fun emoji flair

### Coins, Points, and Rewards
How to earn:
- Win the puzzle (base points)
- Fewer guesses = more bonus points
- Keep a daily streak for extra rewards
- Hard mode and multiplayer wins give extra boosts

What you can unlock:
- New avatars and collectible badges
- Cosmetic themes and frames for your profile
- Power‑ups (single-player only, carefully balanced):
  - Reveal one correct letter
  - Remove a wrong letter from consideration
  - Extra guess chance

### Fair Play
- Competitive modes will include basic protections to keep things fair
- Challenge links expire and can’t be abused

### Rollout Plan (simple)
1) Profiles + basic stats and achievements
2) Daily rewards + coin/points system + store for avatars/badges
3) Leaderboards and friend competitions
4) Challenges (share a word) and social sharing improvements
5) Multiplayer: head‑to‑head first, team mode next

### Success Looks Like
- You feel recognized for your effort (streaks, stats, achievements)
- You have reasons to return daily (rewards, leaderboards, friends)
- The game stays quick, fair, and delightful — with optional depth for power users

### Questions We’ll Decide Together
- Exact “hard mode” rules
- Streak forgiveness (vacations, weekends)
- Which power‑ups feel helpful but not too strong

Thanks for playing WordPop — more playful updates are on the way! 🎉
